# My favorite Series/Movies
1.**Breaking Bad**
My favorite TV series is Breaking Bad. It is a crime drama series about a chemistry teacher named Walter White who gets diagnosed with cancer. Because he wants to secure his family's financial future, he starts making illegal drugs with his former student, Jesse Pinkman. I like this series because it has an interesting story, unexpected twists, and amazing characters. It also shows how a person's decisions can change their life.
2.**John Wick**
My favorite movie is John Wick. It is an action movie about a retired hitman named John Wick who returns to his dangerous past after a tragic event involving his dog. I like this movie because of its intense action scenes, impressive fighting choreography, and interesting storyline. John Wick is also a strong and skilled character who never gives up.
